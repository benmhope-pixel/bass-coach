How to film it
  • Use the Record button here. It films with the front camera so he can see himself, the tuner and the notes, and start it on his own (tap ⟲ for the back camera if someone else is filming). It records at 1080p, 30 fps with the phone's voice processing off, so the tuning is measured cleanly.
  • Landscape, on the stand about 1.5 m away, at chest height, angled from his front-left so the left hand and the fingerboard face the camera.
  • Get head, both arms and the bass down to the bridge in frame. Light on the fingerboard helps the finger tracking.
  • Quiet room, no metronome or drone on speaker during the take. One exercise per clip, at exam tempo.
